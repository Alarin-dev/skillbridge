How It Works

Three steps from beginner to contributor.

1

Build your profile

Tell us what you're learning and where you want to go. Developer, designer, writer, marketer — or just someone ready to start. No prior experience required.

2

Find a project that fits

Browse early-stage startups, community builds, and open ideas looking for contributors. Filter by skill, interest, or time commitment — and apply to the ones that excite you.

3

Contribute and grow

Work with real teams, solve real problems, and walk away with verified portfolio work and honest feedback from people you actually built something with. That's what shows up in interviews.
